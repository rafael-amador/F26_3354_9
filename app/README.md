## Who made which commit

Commits whose message ends with `Co-Authored-By: Claude` were made by the build AI inside the isolated
session (all code under `app/`, `TRACEABILITY.md`, `ASSUMPTIONS.md`, `BUILD_LOG.md`). Commits without it are
the team's setup and proof work (inputs in `spec/`, `proof/`, this README), made by Rafael with help from an AI
assistant working outside the build session; that help is disclosed in `proof/HUMAN_INTERVENTIONS.md`.

## Conditions (confirmed by the instructor)

On 13 Nov 2026 Dr. Barbara Maweu confirmed by email that the bonus conditions are: (1) the app may be
generated with an AI coding tool, (2) the AI may only be given our own deliverables (Proposal, Deliverable #1,
Deliverable #2 material), and (3) we show proof that only those were given to it; she added that the
README must include a citation of the prompt (above).

## Human input

The AI did the implementation. The team owns the project, so team members may correct the AI's assumptions
and clarify use cases. Every such decision is written down as its own input file in `spec/03-team-decisions/`,
hashed in `proof/MANIFEST.sha256` and committed before the AI acts on it, and listed in
`proof/HUMAN_INTERVENTIONS.md`. Nothing reaches the AI except files in `spec/`.
