# TD-3: Water refill map is out of scope

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Team 9 (approved by Rafael Amador, project manager)
- **Changes:** Clarifies a conflict between the Proposal and Deliverable #1.

## Decision
The Proposal (Section 3, Project Objectives) mentions "a map showing the different and closest areas to refill their water." This feature was not carried into the Deliverable #1 functional requirements (FR-1 to FR-8) or use cases (UC-1 to UC-4). The team confirms it is **out of scope** for this version: do not build it. Record it in ASSUMPTIONS.md as an intentionally excluded Proposal feature.

## Why
The team narrowed scope to the core tracking features when requirements were finalized. This change is explained in the report's Conclusion (Section 6).
